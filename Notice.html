Formatting Guide
Numbering and Bulleting
Start a line with # for a numbered point (1), ## for a sub-point ((a)), ### for a sub-sub-point ((i)), or * for a bullet.
Paragraph Spacing
~ removes the indent for that paragraph, ~~ removes the gap before it, ~~~ removes both.
Text Formatting
**text** for bold, __text__ for underline. Combine both with **__text__**.
